Waqt – Daily Islamic Prayer Times

Waqt is a simple Islamic prayer time web app that helps you find daily Salah times based on your location. View Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha prayer times in one place.

You can use your current location or enter a city manually to get prayer times for your area. Waqt also lets you choose different calculation methods and the Asr juristic method according to your preference.

Prayer Times for Your Location

Check today's Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha timings, see the next upcoming prayer, and view a monthly prayer timetable for your selected location.

Islamic Resources and Tools

Waqt brings useful Islamic resources and tools together in one place, including Quran, Duas, Hadith, Islamic Calendar, Qibla and a Zakat Calculator.
