How to use this

This shows the Qibla bearing measured clockwise from true North. Hold your phone flat, open a separate compass app to find true North, then rotate yourself so the Kaaba icon points in the bearing direction shown above.

Note: this is a calculated bearing, not a live magnetic compass — it doesn't use your device's built-in sensor.
